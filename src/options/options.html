Privacy

Hearsay has no servers and no account. Your settings and site commands stay in this browser.

Speech is turned into text by Chrome. With on-device recognition it never leaves your computer; with Chrome’s online service, Google processes the audio the same way it does for voice search.

Hearsay only listens while its side panel is open. While paused it waits for “resume” and ignores everything else.

Gemini Nano runs on your computer. The page outline it sees when drafting code stays on your computer too.
