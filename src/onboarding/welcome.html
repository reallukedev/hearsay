Talk to the board.

Say moves like “e2 to e4” on chess.com. Give any other site its own voice commands. Three steps and you’re playing.

  1. 1

    Allow the microphone

    Chrome asks once. Hearsay listens only while its panel is open.

  2. 2

    Open Hearsay beside the page

    It lives in Chrome’s side panel. Next time, use the toolbar icon or Alt Shift H. Pin the icon from the puzzle-piece menu so it’s always there.

  3. 3

    Say your first move

    Open a board and say “e2 to e4”. Press Alt Shift V any time to pause or resume.
